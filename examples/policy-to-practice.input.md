# Sample Policy Excerpt (Synthetic / Public Standard)

## Trust Policy: Escalation of the Deteriorating Adult Patient (v4.2)

### Section 4.1: NEWS2 Trigger Thresholds
- NEWS2 score 1-4: Low risk. Minimum 4-hourly observations. Registered nurse informed.
- NEWS2 score 5-6 or single parameter scoring 3: Medium risk. Urgent clinical review within 30 minutes by doctor/advanced practitioner. Increase frequency to minimum 1-hourly observations.
- NEWS2 score 7 or more: High risk. Emergency response. Immediate escalation to Resuscitation / Critical Care Outreach Team (CCOT). Continuous physiological monitoring.

### Section 5.2: Escalation Communication
- All escalations MUST use SBAR (Situation, Background, Assessment, Recommendation) framework.
- Time of escalation and recipient identity must be documented in electronic health record within 15 minutes of call.
