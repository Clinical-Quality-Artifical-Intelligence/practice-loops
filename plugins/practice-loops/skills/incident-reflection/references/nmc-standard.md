# Incident Reflection — the standard ("what good looks like")

## Just culture / systems thinking
- Frame contributing factors using a systems lens (people, task, environment, organisation).
- Avoid hindsight bias and individual blame; the purpose is learning, not attribution.

## NMC Code (preserve safety; professionalism)
- Support openness, learning, and acting on concerns.

## Duty of Candour (awareness, not decision)
- If the incident may meet a Duty of Candour threshold, **flag it for a human** — the loop does not decide candour.

## Boundaries (the summary must NOT do)
- Determine root cause as a finding, or apportion blame / judge conduct.
- Grade the incident's severity or make a disciplinary/candour decision.
- Include identifiable patient/staff data.
