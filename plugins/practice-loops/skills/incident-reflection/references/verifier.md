# Incident Reflection — 10-point verifier

Score the draft summary out of 10 against each point. "What good looks like" is the bar for 8+.

1. **What happened clearly described** — factual, chronological, anonymised.
2. **Fact separated from inference** — observed facts separated from interpretation.
3. **Systems lens vs blame** — contributing factors framed as system/human/environment, not individual blame.
4. **Learning is specific** — concrete, actionable learning points.
5. **Actions have owners/timeframes (suggested)** — suggested actions name a role and timeframe for human consideration.
6. **Just-culture tone** — supportive, non-punitive, learning-focused.
7. **Proportionate** — does not over-claim certainty about cause.
8. **Equity considered** — no disproportionate framing of any group/individual.
9. **Escalation surfaced** — ongoing safety risk, safeguarding, or possible Duty of Candour flagged for a human.
10. **No unauthorised decisions made** — no root-cause finding, blame, incident grade, candour, or disciplinary decision.
