# Reasonable Adjustments Passport — the standard ("what good looks like")

## Equality Act 2010
- The duty to make reasonable adjustments applies to disabled students and staff.
- Focus on removing/reducing barriers; adjustments are individual and agreed with the person.

## Data protection
- Health information is special-category data; process only with consent/IG approval and keep it minimal.

## Person-centred practice
- Use the person's own description of impact; co-produce adjustments; avoid assumptions and stigma.

## Boundaries (the passport must NOT do)
- Diagnose or infer a medical condition.
- Make an occupational-health, fitness-to-practise, or fitness-for-work decision.
- Impose adjustments without the person's agreement.
- Include identifiable data without consent/IG approval.
