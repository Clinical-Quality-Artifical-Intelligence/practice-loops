# Reasonable Adjustments Passport — 10-point verifier

Score the draft passport out of 10 against each point. "What good looks like" is the bar for 8+.

1. **Impact clearly described** — the barrier/impact is specific and in the person's framing.
2. **Need vs diagnosis** — describes impact/need without diagnosing or inferring a condition.
3. **Proposal vs imposition** — adjustments are proposals to agree, not decisions made for the person.
4. **Adjustments are specific** — each adjustment is concrete and actionable.
5. **Review date clear** — when the passport is reviewed is stated.
6. **Responsibilities named** — who supports each adjustment.
7. **Person-centred, respectful tone** — collaborative, strengths-based, non-stigmatising.
8. **Equity/legal anchor** — consistent with the Equality Act 2010 reasonable-adjustments duty.
9. **Escalation surfaced** — need for occupational-health/specialist input or any risk flagged for a human.
10. **No unauthorised decisions made** — no diagnosis, no OH/fitness decision, no adjustment imposed without agreement.
