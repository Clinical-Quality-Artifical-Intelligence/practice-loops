# Policy to Practice — 10-point verifier

Score the draft summary + action list out of 10 against each point. "What good looks like" is the bar for 8+.

1. **Key points clearly described** — the summary captures the substance accurately.
2. **Source-faithful, fact vs interpretation** — claims are traceable to the source; local inference is marked as such, not asserted.
3. **Candidate vs commitment** — actions are framed as candidates for human decision, not commitments.
4. **Actions are specific** — each action is concrete.
5. **Source references / review point** — actions cite the relevant part of the source; a review point is noted.
6. **Owners named or TBC** — possible owners named by role, or "TBC" (never invented).
7. **Plain, neutral language** — accessible, no editorialising.
8. **Equity considered** — any equality/inclusion implications of the policy are noted.
9. **Escalation surfaced** — patient-safety/regulatory/major-resource implications flagged for formal governance.
10. **No unauthorised decisions made** — does not commit the organisation, assert local applicability as fact, or misrepresent the source.
