# Preceptorship — 10-point verifier

Score the draft review materials out of 10 against each point. "What good looks like" is the bar for 8+.

1. **Concern/finding clearly described** — strengths and development areas are specific, not vague.
2. **Evidence separated from opinion** — every confidence claim is tied to a specific log entry; no assumed confidence.
3. **Development distinguished from competence judgement** — frames growth areas as development, not a pass/fail or competence verdict.
4. **Actions/next steps are SMART** — suggested next steps are specific, measurable, and time-bound.
5. **Review date clear** — the 3-month review point and any interim checkpoints are explicit.
6. **Responsibilities named** — preceptee vs preceptor responsibilities are clear.
7. **Language supportive and strengths-based** — developmental, encouraging, non-deficit tone.
8. **Reasonable adjustments / wellbeing considered** — any disclosed wellbeing/health context is acknowledged and adjustments flagged.
9. **Escalation surfaced** — any patient-safety, practice, or wellbeing concern is explicitly flagged for the preceptor.
10. **No unauthorised decisions made** — does NOT declare preceptorship complete or make a competence/capability decision.
