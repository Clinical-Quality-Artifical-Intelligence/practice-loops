# Preceptorship — the standard ("what good looks like")

## National preceptorship framework / principles
- Preceptorship is a period of structured support for newly qualified practitioners — not a reassessment of registration.
- Materials support a supportive, developmental conversation and continued learning.

## NMC Code (themes)
- Prioritise people, practise effectively, preserve safety, promote professionalism and trust.
- Frame development against continued proficiency and safe practice.

## Strengths-based approach
- Lead with evidenced strengths; frame gaps as development opportunities with support.
- Confidence claims must be backed by specific evidence from the logs.

## Wellbeing & inclusion
- Acknowledge any disclosed wellbeing/health context; consider reasonable adjustments (Equality Act 2010).
- Keep tone encouraging and psychologically safe.

## Boundaries (the materials must NOT do)
- Declare preceptorship complete or signed off.
- Make a competence, capability, or fitness-to-practise decision.
- Assume confidence or progress the logs do not evidence — mark gaps "to confirm with preceptee".
