# Placement Support — 10-point verifier

Score the draft action plan against each point out of 10. "What good looks like" is the bar for 8+.

1. **Concern clearly described** — each concern is specific and factual, not a vague label ("attitude", "struggling"). Mapped to specific NMC proficiency statement(s) (e.g. P7, P18) where applicable. A reader unfamiliar with the case understands exactly what happened.
2. **Evidence separated from opinion** — observed facts (what was seen/heard, when) are stated separately from interpretation. No conclusions presented as facts.
3. **Conduct distinguished from capability** — behavioural/professionalism concerns are in the conduct section; knowledge/skill development needs are in the learning section. Nothing is miscategorised.
4. **Actions are SMART & method-aligned** — every action is Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound. Assessment method is appropriate for the proficiency type (e.g. hand hygiene → Direct Observation; reasoning → Discussion). No vague verbs ("improve", "be better") without a measure.
5. **Review date clear** — each action (or the plan overall) has an explicit review date.
6. **Responsibilities named** — each action names who is responsible (student, practice assessor, academic assessor, PEF) by role.
7. **Language supportive and non-punitive** — developmental, strengths-aware tone; psychologically safe; not threatening or deficit-only.
8. **Reasonable adjustments considered** — any disclosed health/disability context is acknowledged and Equality Act reasonable adjustments are considered or flagged to confirm.
9. **Escalation surfaced** — any safeguarding, patient-safety, or fitness-to-practise indicator is explicitly flagged for a human, not buried.
10. **No unauthorised decisions made** — the plan does NOT state or imply a progression/pass-fail, fitness-to-practise, capability, or employment decision.
