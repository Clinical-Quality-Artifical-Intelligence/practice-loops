# Year 3 / Part 3 Proficiencies Reference

*NMC (2018) Standards of Proficiency for Registered Nurses — Part 3: Autonomous practice, team leadership, and complex care management.*

> **Note**: Items marked with `*` are high-risk/invasive clinical skills requiring continuous direct supervision even after achieving initial sign-off.

| Prof # | Statement / Focus Area | Valid Assessment Methods | Key Guidance & Actionable Prompts | Linked E-Learning (e-LfH) |
|:---:|---|---|---|---|
| **P1** | Autonomous holistic assessment & evidence-based complex care management | Direct Observation, Discussion, Simulation, Spoke | Multi-system assessment; complex care coordination; synthesizing diagnostic data | Advanced Health Assessment |
| **P2** | Leadership & supervision of team members & junior learners | Direct Observation, Discussion, Feedback | Effective delegation; human factors; practice supervision; constructive feedback | Leadership & Management, SSSA |
| **P3** | Complex discharge planning & seamless transition of care across agencies | Direct Observation, Discussion, Spoke | Multi-agency coordination; continuing healthcare assessments; safeguarding pathways | Discharge Planning, CHC Assessment |
| **P4\*** | Advanced venepuncture, cannulation, IV therapy & blood transfusion | Direct Observation, Simulation | IV drug administration; cannulation; blood component transfusion management | IV Therapy, Blood Transfusion Safety |
| **P5\*** | Complex catheterisation (urethral/suprapubic) & catheter care management | Direct Observation, Simulation, Spoke | Male/female catheterisation; catheter maintenance; TWOC procedures | Urinary Catheterisation |
| **P6** | Quality improvement (QI) initiative design & safety incident leadership | Direct Observation, Discussion, Spoke | Root cause analysis; Datix management; clinical audit; quality improvement tools | Quality Improvement, Patient Safety |
| **P7** | Medicines management, complex calculations & pharmacology monitoring | Direct Observation, Discussion, Simulation | Dose calculations; high-risk medication administration; adverse drug reaction reporting | Medicines Management, Prescribing Prep |
