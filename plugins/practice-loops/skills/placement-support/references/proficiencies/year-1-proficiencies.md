# Year 1 / Part 1 Proficiencies Reference

*NMC (2018) Standards of Proficiency for Registered Nurses — Part 1: Guided participation in care with increasing confidence.*

| Prof # | Statement / Focus Area | Valid Assessment Methods | Key Guidance & Actionable Prompts | Linked E-Learning (e-LfH) |
|:---:|---|---|---|---|
| **P2** | Understanding of age & development across lifespan in nursing assessment | Direct Observation, Discussion, Simulation, Spoke, Feedback | Adjust verbal/non-verbal communication for age/cognition; identify communication barriers; involve advocates | Person Centred Approaches |
| **P3** | Process assessment info to identify fundamental care needs & care plans | Direct Observation, Discussion, Simulation, Spoke | Complete holistic admission; record clinical observations; follow EWS escalation policy; document daily notes | Person Centred Approaches, Breaking Down Barriers |
| **P4** | Work in partnership for shared decision-making and self-care | Direct Observation, Discussion, Feedback, Simulation, Spoke | Best interest meetings, discharge planning, social care spoke; encourage independence in ADLs | Shared Decision Making, Making Every Contact Count (MECC) |
| **P5** | Therapeutic relationships (MH, behavioural, cognitive, learning challenges) | Direct Observation, Discussion, Simulation, Spoke | Professional boundaries; active listening; advance care plans; adapt for delusions/dementia | MCA, Communication Skills for MH, Mindfulness |
| **P6** | Person-centred care for anxiety, confusion, pain, breathlessness | Direct Observation, Discussion, Simulation, Spoke | Empathic questioning; pain scoring tools; relaxation/mindfulness techniques; safety plans | Person Centred Approaches, Breaking Down Barriers |
| **P7** | Prompt action on signs of deterioration or distress (mental, physical, cognitive) | Direct Observation, Discussion, Simulation, Spoke | Vital signs & EWS escalation; falls risk; DASA (aggression assessment); report baseline variances | Recognising Deterioration, Sepsis |
| **P8** | Assess comfort levels, rest, and sleep patterns | Direct Observation, Discussion, Simulation, Spoke | Sleep hygiene techniques; sleep diaries; pain assessment; environment management | Person Centred Approaches |
| **P9** | Privacy & dignity promotion to encourage rest, sleep, and self-care | Direct Observation, Discussion, Spoke | Collaborative questioning; maintain privacy during personal care; bed space allocation | Person Centred Approaches |
| **P10** | Assess skin & hygiene status; tissue viability needs | Direct Observation, Discussion, Simulation, Spoke | Pressure sore risk tools (Waterlow/Braden); body mapping; wound assessment; emollient care | Essentials of Wound Care, E-dermatology |
| **P11** | Assist with washing, bathing, shaving, dressing & bed making | Direct Observation, Simulation, Spoke | Assisted bathing; oral healthcare; menstrual hygiene support; infection control bed making | Supporting Self Care (SSC) |
| **P12** | Support diet & nutritional needs, taking cultural practices into account | Direct Observation, Discussion, Simulation, Spoke | MUST tool calculation; BMI calculation; dysphagia support; choking risk assessment | Dysphagia Guide, Nutrition & Obesity |
| **P13** | Recognize signs/symptoms of dehydration & fluid retention; fluid balance | Direct Observation, Discussion, Simulation, Spoke | Fluid balance charts; height/weight tracking; fluid targets & restrictions | Continence and Catheter Care |
| **P14** | Assist with toileting, maintaining dignity, privacy & using aids | Direct Observation, Discussion, Simulation, Spoke | Safe toileting assistance; waste disposal; continence aids; urine output tracking | Continence and Catheter Care |
| **P15** | Select and use continence & feminine hygiene products appropriately | Direct Observation, Discussion, Simulation, Spoke | Baseline continence assessment; appropriate product selection; safe disposal | Continence and Catheter Care |
| **P16** | Assess mobility support needs & promote independence | Direct Observation, Discussion, Simulation, Spoke | Mobility equipment (frames, wheelchairs, hoists); skin checks; falls risk assessment | Frailty, Preventing Falls in Hospitals |
| **P17** | Moving & handling techniques & equipment safety | Direct Observation, Discussion, Simulation, Spoke | Safe hoist/sling/pat-slide operation; deconditioning syndrome prevention | Frailty, Preventing Falls in Hospitals |
| **P18** | Consistently utilize evidence-based hand washing techniques | Direct Observation | 5 Moments for Hand Hygiene; WHO handwashing steps; IPC audits | IPC / Hand Hygiene |
| **P19** | Identify potential infection risks & utilize PPE correctly | Direct Observation, Discussion, Simulation, Spoke | Donning/doffing PPE; face fit testing; infection control guidelines | Cleaning for Confidence, AMR & Infections |
| **P20** | Safe decontamination & disposal of waste, laundry, and sharps | Direct Observation, Discussion, Spoke | Segregation of clinical vs non-clinical waste; safe sharps handling & disposal | IPC / Cleaning for Confidence |
| **P21** | Manual & electronic measurement & recording of vital signs | Direct Observation, Discussion, Simulation, Spoke | Pulse oximetry, manual BP, temperature measurement; EWS escalation | Recognising Deterioration |
| **P22** | Measure weight/height, calculate BMI & recognize clinical significance | Direct Observation, Discussion, Simulation, Spoke | Socioeconomic food factors; accurate BMI tracking; medical context | Obesity (BMI) |
| **P23** | Collect & observe sputum, urine, stool specimens & interpret findings | Direct Observation, Simulation, Spoke | Specimen pot selection; urine dipstick testing; blood bottle selection | IPC / Diagnostic Tools |
| **P24** | Person-centred risk assessments (suicide, aggression, falls, MUST) | Direct Observation, Discussion, Simulation, Spoke | Risk mitigation planning; DASA; suicide risk; safety plan documentation | Person Centred Approaches, SSC |
| **P25** | Health & safety regulations & proactive hazard response | Direct Observation, Discussion, Simulation, Spoke | Identify physical/chemical/biological hazards; incident reporting; trust policy | Health & Safety / Safety Reporting |
| **P26** | Multi-agency & interprofessional partnership working | Direct Observation, Discussion, Spoke | Interprofessional handovers; MDT meetings; transfer of care documentation | Multi-agency Working |
| **P27** | Manage challenges of co-morbidities (physical, psychological, social) | Direct Observation, Discussion, Spoke | Bio-psycho-social care model; complex care planning; referral pathways | Co-morbidity Care |
| **P28** | Support independence for individuals, carers, and families | Direct Observation, Discussion, Spoke | Discharge planning; community referral pathways; supported living | Person Centred Care |
| **P29** | Accurate verbal, digital, or written handover documentation | Direct Observation, Discussion, Simulation, Spoke | Timely record keeping; ward rounds & shift handovers; info governance | IT Skills / Record Keeping |
