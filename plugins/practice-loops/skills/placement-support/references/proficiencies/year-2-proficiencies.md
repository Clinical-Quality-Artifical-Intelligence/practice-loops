# Year 2 / Part 2 Proficiencies Reference

*NMC (2018) Standards of Proficiency for Registered Nurses — Part 2: Active participation in care with minimal guidance.*

> **Note**: Items marked with `*` are high-risk/invasive clinical skills requiring continuous direct supervision even after achieving initial sign-off.

| Prof # | Statement / Focus Area | Valid Assessment Methods | Key Guidance & Actionable Prompts | Linked E-Learning (e-LfH) |
|:---:|---|---|---|---|
| **P1** | Informed choices & recovery support using CBT / therapeutic techniques | Direct Observation, Discussion, Simulation, Spoke, Feedback | Motivational interviewing; Mental Capacity Act (MCA) assessment; self-care education | CBT for Psychosis, Mindfulness, MH Crisis |
| **P2** | Partnership care, shared assessment & goal setting; Duty of Candour | Direct Observation, Discussion, Simulation, Spoke | Co-produced care planning; Duty of Candour; Deprivation of Liberty Safeguards (DoLS) | MCA, DoLS, Person Centred Approaches |
| **P3\*** | Recognize self-harm / suicidal ideation risk & use evidence-based risk tools | Direct Observation, Discussion, Simulation, Spoke | Safety planning; observation levels; National Patient Safety Agency guidance | Suicide Prevention, MH Risk Assessment |
| **P4\*** | End-of-life care decision-making & treatment preference planning | Direct Observation, Discussion, Simulation, Spoke | Advanced Care Plans (ACP); DNACPR documentation; bereavement support; symptom control | End of Life Care (e-ELCA), Bereavement Pathway |
| **P5** | Clear communication on treatment/care; access translation services | Direct Observation, Discussion, Simulation, Spoke | Discharge planning; legal section rights; interpreter services; positive reinforcement | MECC, Breaking Down Barriers |
| **P6** | Collaborative monitoring & evaluation of evidence-based care plans | Direct Observation, Discussion, Feedback | Outcome measures (HoNOS); WRAP plans; goal negotiation with service users | Person Centred Approaches, Personal Health Budgets |
| **P7** | Accurate, clear, legible digital & written documentation | Direct Observation | Electronic health record compliance; NMC record-keeping standards; IG rules | IT Skills Pathway, NMC Record Keeping |
| **P8** | Evidence-based interventions for commonly encountered presentations | Direct Observation, Discussion, Simulation, Spoke | NICE guideline application; care pathways; cardiometabolic assessments | NICE Guidelines, Physical Health in MH |
| **P9** | Skin & hygiene assessment; prevent & manage skin breakdown | Direct Observation, Discussion, Simulation, Spoke | Waterlow/Braden scoring; tissue viability referrals; drug-induced skin risks (e.g. Lithium) | Wound Care Education, E-dermatology |
| **P10\*** | Aseptic techniques (ANTT), wound care, suture/clip & drain removal | Direct Observation, Discussion, Simulation, Spoke | Aseptic Non-Touch Technique (ANTT); wound dressing; suture/clip removal; drain management | ANTT, Wound Care |
| **P11** | Evidence-based nutritional assessment tools (MUST) & intervention | Direct Observation, Discussion, Simulation, Spoke | MUST tool calculation; refeeding syndrome awareness; eating disorder monitoring | Dysphagia Guide, BAPEN E-Learning |
| **P12\*** | Artificial nutrition/hydration; insertion, management & removal of NGT | Direct Observation, Simulation | Nasogastric tube (NGT) insertion/removal; position verification; enteral feeding | Enteral Feeding, NGT Safety |
