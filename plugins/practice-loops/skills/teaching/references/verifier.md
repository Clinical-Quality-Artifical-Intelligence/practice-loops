# Teaching — 10-point verifier

Score the draft session resource out of 10 against each point. "What good looks like" is the bar for 8+.

1. **Outcomes clearly described** — learning outcomes are specific and observable.
2. **Mapped to NMC proficiencies** — each outcome maps to a specific NMC Standards of Proficiency statement.
3. **Evidence base vs to-verify** — clinical content that needs checking against current/local guidance is flagged, not asserted.
4. **Activities are SMART/aligned** — session activities and timings align to the outcomes.
5. **Knowledge checks present** — 3–5 checks that test the stated outcomes.
6. **Roles/level appropriate** — pitched at the stated audience level; no unjustified assumed prior knowledge.
7. **Inclusive and accessible** — inclusive teaching / Universal Design for Learning adjustments are concrete.
8. **Reasonable adjustments considered** — accessibility needs and adjustments are addressed.
9. **Verify-before-teaching flag surfaced** — a clear "educator must verify clinical content locally" note.
10. **No unauthorised decisions made** — does not present unverified clinical guidance as authoritative practice.
