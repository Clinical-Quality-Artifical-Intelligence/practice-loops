# Teaching — the standard ("what good looks like")

## NMC Standards of Proficiency
- Map each learning outcome to specific proficiency numbers (e.g. P7, P18, P21) and Annexe A/B skills from `placement-support/references/proficiencies/`.
- Specify valid assessment and teaching methods for each outcome using `placement-support/references/proficiencies/assessment-methods.md`.

## Evidence base
- Base clinical content on current evidence; explicitly flag anything the educator must verify against current national/local guidelines before teaching.

## Inclusive teaching / Universal Design for Learning (UDL)
- Provide multiple means of engagement, representation, and expression.
- Make reasonable adjustments concrete (Equality Act 2010).

## Pitch & alignment
- Match the stated audience level; align activities and knowledge checks to the learning outcomes.

## Boundaries (the resource must NOT do)
- Present clinical content as definitive without a "verify locally" flag.
- Assume prior knowledge beyond what is stated.
- Replace the educator's professional judgement — it is a DRAFT to adapt and verify.
