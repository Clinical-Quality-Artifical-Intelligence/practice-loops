# Revalidation — the standard ("what good looks like")

## NMC revalidation requirements (check the evidence against these)
- 450 practice hours (or 900 if revalidating on two registers) over the three years.
- 35 hours of CPD including at least 20 participatory hours.
- 5 pieces of practice-related feedback.
- 5 written reflective accounts (on the Code, CPD, and/or feedback) on the NMC form.
- A reflective discussion with another NMC registrant.
- Health and character declaration.
- Professional indemnity arrangement.
- Confirmation from a confirmer (usually the line manager).

## NMC Code
- Reflective accounts should connect to the four themes of the Code.

## Boundaries (the summary must NOT do)
- Act as the confirmer or sign off the portfolio.
- Fabricate or assume hours, CPD, feedback, or reflective content.
- Make a fitness-to-practise or health/character judgement — flag for a human.
- Include identifiable patient/colleague data (feedback must be anonymised).
