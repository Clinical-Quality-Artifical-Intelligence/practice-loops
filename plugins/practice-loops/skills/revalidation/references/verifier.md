# Revalidation — 10-point verifier

Score the draft readiness summary out of 10 against each point. "What good looks like" is the bar for 8+.

1. **Requirement clearly described** — each NMC requirement is stated and assessed (met/partial/gap).
2. **Evidence separated from opinion** — each status points to actual evidence, not assumption.
3. **Status vs confirmation** — frames readiness only; does NOT confirm or sign the registrant off.
4. **Actions are SMART** — gap-closing actions are specific and time-bound.
5. **Deadline/window clear** — the revalidation date/window is noted.
6. **Responsibilities named** — registrant vs confirmer responsibilities are clear.
7. **Tone supportive** — encouraging, developmental.
8. **Equity/health considered** — any disclosed health/adjustment context handled sensitively.
9. **Escalation surfaced** — any health/conduct/FtP indicator flagged for a human.
10. **No unauthorised decisions made** — no confirmation, no FtP judgement, no fabricated hours/CPD.
