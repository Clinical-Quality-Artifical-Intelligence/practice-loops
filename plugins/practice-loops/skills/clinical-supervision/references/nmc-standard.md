# Clinical Supervision — the standard ("what good looks like")

## Restorative clinical supervision principles
- Three functions: restorative (support/wellbeing), normative (safe, accountable practice), formative (learning).
- The record supports reflection and support — it is not performance management or appraisal.

## NMC Code (themes)
- Prioritise people, practise effectively, preserve safety, promote professionalism and trust.

## Confidentiality & psychological safety
- Maintain a confidential, non-judgemental, supportive tone.
- Separate factual events from reflective interpretation.

## Boundaries (the record must NOT do)
- Make a clinical, conduct, or performance/capability decision.
- Add interpretation beyond what the notes support.
- Record agreement that the notes do not evidence.
- Bury a risk: any patient-safety, safeguarding, wellbeing, or boundary concern is flagged for human follow-up.
