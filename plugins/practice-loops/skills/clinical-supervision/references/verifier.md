# Clinical Supervision — 10-point verifier

Score the draft follow-up record out of 10 against each point. "What good looks like" is the bar for 8+.

1. **Themes clearly described** — themes are specific and drawn only from the notes.
2. **Evidence separated from opinion** — what was said/observed is separated from interpretation; nothing invented.
3. **Restorative vs corrective framing** — framed restoratively (support, learning), not as performance management.
4. **Actions are SMART** — agreed actions are specific, measurable, and time-bound.
5. **Review/next-session point clear** — when the follow-up happens is explicit.
6. **Responsibilities named** — who does what is clear.
7. **Language supportive and non-punitive** — confidential, non-judgemental, psychologically safe tone.
8. **Wellbeing considered** — supervisee wellbeing is acknowledged where relevant.
9. **Escalation surfaced** — any patient risk, safeguarding, wellbeing, or boundary concern is flagged for a human.
10. **No unauthorised decisions made** — does NOT make a clinical, conduct, or performance decision; records "agreed" only where the notes show agreement.
