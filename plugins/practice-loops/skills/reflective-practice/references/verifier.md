# Reflective Practice — 10-point verifier

Score the draft reflective scaffold out of 10 against each point. "What good looks like" is the bar for 8+.

1. **Experience clearly described** — the situation is specific and anonymised.
2. **Evidence/fact vs feeling** — events are separated from interpretation and emotion.
3. **Reflection vs others' conduct** — focuses on the registrant's own learning, not judging others.
4. **Learning is specific** — a concrete learning point and action emerge.
5. **Action has a timeframe** — the action/next step is time-bound.
6. **Ownership prompts present** — prompts invite the registrant's own words (not pre-written for them).
7. **Tone reflective and honest** — supportive, non-defensive.
8. **Code themes mapped** — links to the relevant NMC Code themes.
9. **Escalation surfaced** — any safety/safeguarding/FtP concern flagged for a human.
10. **No unauthorised decisions made** — no fabricated content; no conclusions about others' fitness/conduct.
