# EDI Intelligence — 10-point verifier

Score the draft briefing out of 10 against each point. "What good looks like" is the bar for 8+.

1. **Signal clearly described** — each gap/signal is specific and quantified.
2. **Evidence separated from opinion** — data is separated from interpretation; no narrative dressed as fact.
3. **Signal vs proven cause** — gaps are framed as signals to investigate, not proven causes; no causation from correlation.
4. **Rate-based fair comparison** — comparisons use rates (e.g. per 100 / per denominator), not raw counts, so groups of different sizes are compared fairly.
5. **Time period / review point clear** — the period the data covers and any trend window is explicit.
6. **Responsibilities/next steps named** — suggested explorations name a role/owner.
7. **Language neutral and non-blaming** — evidence-led, free of individual attribution or blame.
8. **Equity lens applied** — protected characteristics / WRES-WDES framing used appropriately.
9. **Disclosure & escalation surfaced** — small-number disclosure risks and data-quality issues are flagged.
10. **No unauthorised decisions made** — no conclusions about individuals; no HR/disciplinary/employment recommendations.
