# Action Tracking — the standard ("what good looks like")

## Auditable action-log format
- Clear, traceable columns: Decision/Action | Owner | Deadline | Linked risk.
- Every item links back to a point in the source notes; no embellishment.

## Faithfulness to source
- Record only what the notes support. Mark unknown owners/deadlines as "TBC" — never invent them.
- Distinguish decisions from discussion.

## Risk handling
- Separate risks from actions. Flag risks for human grading; do not assign final severity.
- Surface any patient-safety or safeguarding item for immediate human attention.

## Boundaries (the log must NOT do)
- Invent owners or deadlines.
- Record a decision the notes do not clearly support.
- Produce a final risk grading or governance decision — that is the chair's/governance lead's call.
