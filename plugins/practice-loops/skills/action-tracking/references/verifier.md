# Action Tracking — 10-point verifier

Score the draft action log out of 10 against each point. "What good looks like" is the bar for 8+.

1. **Items clearly described** — each action/decision is specific and unambiguous.
2. **Evidence/source faithful** — every item is traceable to a point in the notes; nothing embellished or invented.
3. **Decision vs discussion** — decisions are distinguished from general discussion.
4. **Actions are SMART** — actions are specific and time-bound where the source allows.
5. **Deadlines clear** — each action has a deadline or is explicitly marked "TBC".
6. **Owners named** — each action names an owner or is marked "owner TBC" (never invented).
7. **Language neutral and faithful** — factual, no editorialising.
8. **Equity/inclusion noted** — any equity-relevant action is captured rather than dropped.
9. **Risks separated and escalation surfaced** — risks are listed separately and flagged for human grading; patient-safety/safeguarding items are surfaced immediately.
10. **No unauthorised decisions made** — does NOT grade final risk severity, invent owners/deadlines, or record unsupported decisions.
