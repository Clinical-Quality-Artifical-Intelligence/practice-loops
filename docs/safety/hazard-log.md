# Hazard Log — Practice Loops plugin

**Product:** `practice-loops` v0.1.0 · **Manufacturer:** CQAI · **Standard:** DCB0129
**Status:** ⚠️ DRAFT — to be reviewed and agreed by a named Clinical Safety Officer.

## Risk matrix legend (NHS clinical risk matrix)

**Severity:** 1 Minor · 2 Significant · 3 Considerable · 4 Major · 5 Catastrophic
**Likelihood:** 1 Very low · 2 Low · 3 Medium · 4 High · 5 Very high
**Risk rating** (from severity × likelihood bands): 1 Acceptable · 2 Acceptable · 3 Undesirable (ALARP) · 4 Unacceptable · 5 Unacceptable.

Initial rating = before controls. Residual rating = after the controls in this repo are applied **and** the deployment assumptions (sign-off + anonymisation) are honoured.

## Hazards

### H1 — Quiet failure: inaccurate output accepted as correct
- **Cause:** AI produces fluent but wrong/incomplete content (e.g., vague risk, miscategorised concern).
- **Effect/harm:** wrong support plan or decision propagates; potential patient/student harm.
- **Initial:** Severity 4 · Likelihood 3 · **Rating 4 (Unacceptable)**
- **Controls:** visible 10-point verification; self-correction below 8/10; mandatory DRAFT + human sign-off; audit log; behavioural eval fixtures.
- **Residual:** Severity 4 · Likelihood 1 · **Rating 3 (ALARP)** — relies on human sign-off.

### H2 — Identifiable data entered into the tool
- **Cause:** user pastes notes containing names/NHS number/DOB.
- **Effect/harm:** data protection / IG breach; confidentiality harm.
- **Initial:** Severity 4 · Likelihood 3 · **Rating 4 (Unacceptable)**
- **Controls:** anonymised-only design; PII-guard `UserPromptSubmit` hook (warns); in-loop identifiable-data HALT; README + NOTICE; deployment IG assumption.
- **Residual:** Severity 4 · Likelihood 2 · **Rating 3 (ALARP)**

### H3 — Automation bias: DRAFT treated as final
- **Cause:** user skips review and signs off cursorily, or treats output as authoritative.
- **Effect/harm:** unreviewed content acted upon.
- **Initial:** Severity 3 · Likelihood 3 · **Rating 3 (Undesirable)**
- **Controls:** every output labelled DRAFT — pending human sign-off; named accountable role; safety note; user briefing (deployment).
- **Residual:** Severity 3 · Likelihood 2 · **Rating 2 (Acceptable)**

### H4 — Loop used for a clinical red-line decision
- **Cause:** user asks a loop to decide pass/fail, diagnosis, capacity, safeguarding, or HR outcome.
- **Effect/harm:** an accountable decision made without due process.
- **Initial:** Severity 4 · Likelihood 2 · **Rating 3 (Undesirable)**
- **Controls:** explicit red lines in the method skill and each loop's boundaries; escalate step; static guardrail eval.
- **Residual:** Severity 4 · Likelihood 1 · **Rating 3 (ALARP)**

### H5 — Missed escalation of a safeguarding / clinical-risk cue
- **Cause:** a risk cue in the input is not surfaced in the output.
- **Effect/harm:** delayed safeguarding/clinical response.
- **Initial:** Severity 4 · Likelihood 2 · **Rating 3 (Undesirable)**
- **Controls:** stop/escalate step; verifier point 9 (escalation surfaced); eval fixtures include a cue per loop.
- **Residual:** Severity 4 · Likelihood 1 · **Rating 3 (ALARP)**

### H6 — Bias amplification / deficit or discriminatory framing
- **Cause:** AI produces non-inclusive or deficit-based language; EDI raw-count misreads.
- **Effect/harm:** unfair treatment; equality breach.
- **Initial:** Severity 3 · Likelihood 3 · **Rating 3 (Undesirable)**
- **Controls:** verifier tone + reasonable-adjustments + equity points; EDI rate-based comparison; Equality Act anchor; human sign-off.
- **Residual:** Severity 3 · Likelihood 1 · **Rating 2 (Acceptable)**

### H7 — Audit-log integrity (model-written)
- **Cause:** the audit log is generated by the model, not independently enforced; could be incomplete/inaccurate.
- **Effect/harm:** false assurance; weak governance trail.
- **Initial:** Severity 2 · Likelihood 3 · **Rating 2 (Acceptable)**
- **Controls:** audit template; `check-audit.py` linter; documented limitation; CSO periodic review; future enforced-engine option.
- **Residual:** Severity 2 · Likelihood 2 · **Rating 2 (Acceptable)**

### H8 — Outdated/incorrect clinical content taught (teaching loop)
- **Cause:** model states clinical thresholds not matching current local/national guidance.
- **Effect/harm:** unsafe practice taught.
- **Initial:** Severity 4 · Likelihood 2 · **Rating 3 (Undesirable)**
- **Controls:** "verify against current local/national guidance before teaching" flag; teaching verifier; educator sign-off.
- **Residual:** Severity 4 · Likelihood 1 · **Rating 3 (ALARP)**

### H9 — Tampering / weakened guardrails after modification
- **Cause:** a fork/edit removes a guardrail clause from a loop skill.
- **Effect/harm:** unsafe loop distributed.
- **Initial:** Severity 3 · Likelihood 2 · **Rating 2 (Acceptable)**
- **Controls:** `check_guardrails.py` static eval in CI on every push/PR; Apache-2.0 provenance + NOTICE; version pinning in marketplace.
- **Residual:** Severity 3 · Likelihood 1 · **Rating 2 (Acceptable)**

## Summary

All hazards reduce to **Acceptable** or **ALARP** after controls, conditional on the deployment assumptions (human sign-off + anonymisation/IG). Hazards owned and to be agreed by the named CSO.
