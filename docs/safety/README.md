# Clinical safety (DCB0129)

This folder holds the clinical safety evidence for the `practice-loops` plugin, aligned with
**DCB0129** (clinical risk management for the *manufacture* of health IT systems).

- [`clinical-safety-case.md`](clinical-safety-case.md) — the Clinical Safety Case Report.
- [`hazard-log.md`](hazard-log.md) — the FMEA-style hazard log with the NHS risk matrix.

> ⚠️ **These are manufacturer drafts.** They are not a completed safety case until a named,
> suitably qualified **Clinical Safety Officer** has reviewed them, agreed the hazard log, and
> signed the declaration. Use the `dcb0129`, `hazard-log`, and `crm-plan` skills to extend them.

**Deploying organisations** should additionally produce a local **DCB0160** deployment safety
case (deployment hazards, local configuration, IG approval, staff training).
